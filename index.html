MISChaufen is a virtual hackerspace, a group of friends, colleagues and everything in between. We hang out online all year to share projects, break things, fix them again and swap ideas on tech, security, homelabs, radio and whatever else catches our interest. Not many groups span from exploiting software to soldering antennas — we do.

We're into:

  • IT Security
  • Hardware Tinkering
  • Radio Sports
  • Overengineering things for fun
