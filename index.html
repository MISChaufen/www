About Projects Team Contact

MISChaufen is a virtual hackerspace, a group of friends, colleagues and everything in between. We hang out online all year to share projects, break things, fix them again and swap ideas on tech, security, homelabs, radio and whatever else catches our interest. At 40C3 we finally get to meet in person again — come by for a chat, show us what you're working on, or join the pile.

Come find us if you are into:

  • IT Security
  • Hardware Tinkering
  • Radio Sports
  • having a drink or some smoke
